Station map mein sirf 12 platforms the.
Phir bhi…
Jab Kai ne last staircase descend ki…
uske saamne ek dark hallway tha.
Aur us hallway ke end pe—
“13”
Paint kiya hua tha.
Dim red light blink kar rahi thi.
Platform completely khaali tha.
No guards.
No passengers.
Sirf Cold wind.
Aur tracks ke paas…
3 log khade the.
Sabke haath mein black cards.
Ek tall girl hoodie mein.
Ek banda jiske knuckles pe scars the.
Aur teesra…
White mask pehne hua.
Kai dheere dheere unke paas gaya.
Tabhi station speakers phirse on hue.
“Welcome… Crownless Candidates.”
Lights ek second ke liye band hui.
Aur jab wapas aayi—
Platform ke center mein
woh blood-soaked white shirt wala banda khada tha.
Cold grey eyes.
Same smile.
“12:14,” usne dheere se kaha.
“Perfect timing.”
