“Kai beta, khana rakh du?”
Kai ne bina dekhe kaha,
“Bhuk nahi hai...”
Par uski nazrein abhi bhi us black card par thi.
Uski mom ne card notice kiya...
aur usse utha liya.
“Beta, ye kiska card hai?”
Mom ke ye kehte hi...
Kai ke dimaag mein wohi scene flash hua.
Blood soaked white shirt...
Wet road...
11 bodies...
Aur woh cold grey eyes.
Kai ka body phirse freeze ho gaya.
Uski mom thoda paas aayi.
“Beta... kya hua?”
Kai dheere se bola—
“Vo aadmi...”
“Kon aadmi, beta?”
Kai achanak panic mein aa gaya.
“Mom... usse mat chhuo.”
Uski mom thodi confuse ho gayi... aur halka sa darr bhi gayi.
Kai ne jaldi se card wapas liya... aur khudko calm karne laga.
“Kuch nahi mom... bas thoda tired hu...”
Uski mom ne usse dhyan se dekha.
“Haan beta... par tum ajeeb behave kar rahe ho...”
Kai forced smile deta hai.
“Aapne khana khaya?”
“Haan... par tum bhi kha lo.”
Kai kuch seconds chup raha...
Phir washroom jaake muh dhoya... aur aake chup chaap khana khane laga.
Us raat...
Kai bed pe let ke ceiling ko dekh raha tha.
Uske dimaag mein sirf wahi sawal ghoom raha tha...
“Subway Platform 13 pe aisa kya hai...?”
