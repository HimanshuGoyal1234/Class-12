City ka naam tha Noir Vale.
Din mein normal.
Raat mein jaanwar.
Yahan fights sirf jeetne ke liye nahi hoti thi — survival ke liye hoti thi.
Har area ka apna ruler tha.
Har ruler ke neeche gangs.
Aur har gang ke neeche woh log... jo bas zinda rehna chahte the.
Lekin ek rumor sab jagah same tha.
“Agar midnight ke baad subway platform 13 par koi akela khada mile... toh ya toh woh marne wala hai, ya legend banne wala.”
17-year-old Kai Ren ko legends pe believe nahi tha.
Uske liye life simple thi — school attend karo, part-time kaam karo, aur fights avoid karo.
Par problems usse avoid nahi karti thi.
Ek raat jab woh convenience store band karke ghar ja raha tha, usne road ke beech ek banda dekha.
White shirt blood se soaked.
Face shadows mein hidden.
Aur uske aas paas... 11 bodies.
Dead ya unconscious — samajhna mushkil tha.
Kai freeze ho gaya.
Woh banda slowly uski taraf muda.
Cold grey eyes.
“Time kya hua hai?” usne poocha.
Kai ka gala sookh gaya.
“12... 12:14.”
Banda halka sa smile kiya.
“Late ho gaya.”
Usne apne pocket se ek black card nikala aur Kai ke haath mein rakh diya.
Card pe sirf ek symbol tha—
A broken crown.
Phir next second...
Police sirens.
Kai ne ek second ke liye aankh midhi.
Aur jab wapas dekha—
Woh banda gayab tha.
Bas wet road... blood... aur uske haath mein woh black card bacha tha.
Us raat ke baad, Kai ko samajh aaya—
Noir Vale mein kuch doors ek baar khul jaayein... toh phir kabhi band nahi hote.
