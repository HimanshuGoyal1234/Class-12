Kai apne ghar aaya… bhagta hua.
Uski saansein bohot tez thi.
Kai ne apni zindagi mein pehli baar itna saara blood ek saath dekha tha… aur woh abhi bhi shock mein tha.
Uske haath abhi bhi kaanp rahe the.
Kai ne turant apna PC on kiya aur Zoggle pe search kiya:
“A Broken Crown”
Uske saamne sirf ek link aayi.
“The Crownless”
Kai ne bina soche us link pe click kar diya.
Aur next second…
uska pura desktop black ho gaya.
Kai freeze.
2 second baad screen ke center mein do black eyes appear hui.
Eyes dheere dheere open hui…
Jaise screen ke andar se koi usse dekh raha ho.
Kai ki heartbeat aur fast ho gayi.
2 second tak woh eyes bas usse stare karti rahi…
Phir slowly close ho gayi.
Kai chair se peeche hat gaya.
“Ye kya hai…” usne dheere se kaha.
Usne jaldi se pani piya…
Aur tabhi desktop pe white text type hona start hua.
“Welcome to Broken Crown.”
Kai us line ko dekh hi raha tha ki—
Screen glitch hui.
Website suddenly crash ho gayi.
Ya crash karwa di?
Kai ko kuch samajh nahi aaya.
Usne phirse website open karne ki koshish ki…
Par iss baar sirf ek line aa rahi thi:
“This site can’t be reached.”
Room mein silence tha.
Sirf CPU fan ki awaaz aa rahi thi.
Kai ko ab samajh aa chuka tha…
ye koi normal card nahi hai.
Ye kuch aur hi hai.
