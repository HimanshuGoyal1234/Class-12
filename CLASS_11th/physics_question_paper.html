🔹 Section B (Q19 – Q25) · 2 marks each
Q19
Define angle of friction and angle of repose. Show that both are equal.
✅ Answer: tan θ = μ; angle of friction = angle of repose = tan⁻¹μ.
Q20
State Bernoulli’s principle and give one application.
✅ Answer: For ideal fluid, P + ½ρv² + ρgh = const. Application: aerofoil lift.
Q21
What is the angle between two vectors A and B if |A×B| = A·B ?
✅ Answer: |A×B| = AB sinθ, A·B = AB cosθ → sinθ = cosθ → θ = 45°
Q22
Why does a cricket player lower his hands while catching a ball?
✅ Answer: To increase time, reduce force (impulse = change in momentum = F·Δt).
Q23
Distinguish between elastic and inelastic collision.
✅ Answer: In elastic KE conserved, in inelastic KE not conserved (momentum conserved both).
Q24
Define coefficient of static friction. How is it related to limiting friction?
✅ Answer: μₛ = fₛ(max)/N ; limiting friction = μₛ N.
Q25 a)
If a body has infinite heat capacity, what does it mean?
✅ Answer: Any amount of heat does not change its temperature (e.g., during phase change).
Q25 b)
Define triple point of water.
✅ Answer: Temperature & pressure where solid, liquid, vapour coexist (273.16 K, 611 Pa).

Q35 (Escape velocity)
1. If mass of A larger than B, escape speed of A – (same, less, more?)
✅ Same (independent of object mass).
2. VA (earth) and VB (moon) for identical objects?
✅ VA > VB (earth's gravity higher).
3. Planet radius 6000 km, escape velocity 12 km/s. Find g.
✅ Ve = √(2gR) → (12000)² = 2g×6×10⁶ → g = 12 m/s².
⭐ OR: Earth Ve=11.2 km/s. Planet mass double, radius half → new Ve?
✅ Ve' / 11.2 = √(2×2) = 2 → Ve' = 22.4 km/s.
