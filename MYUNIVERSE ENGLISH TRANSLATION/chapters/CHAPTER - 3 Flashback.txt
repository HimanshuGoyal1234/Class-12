“Kai, beta, should I put out dinner for you?”

Kai answered without looking.  
“Not hungry…”

But his eyes were still fixed on that black card.

His mom noticed the card… and picked it up.  
“Beta, whose card is this?”

The moment she said that —  
that same scene flashed through Kai’s mind.  
The blood-soaked white shirt…  
The wet road…  
11 bodies…  
And those cold grey eyes.

Kai’s body froze again.

His mom moved a little closer.  
“Beta… what happened?”

Kai spoke softly —  
“That man…”

“Which man, beta?”

Kai suddenly started panicking.  
“Mom… don’t touch it.”

His mom got a bit confused… and a little frightened too.

Kai quickly took the card back… and tried to calm himself down.  
“Nothing, Mom… just a bit tired…”

His mom looked at him carefully.  
“Okay, beta… but you’re acting strange…”

Kai forces a smile.  
“Did you eat?”

“Yes… but you should eat too.”

Kai stayed quiet for a few seconds…  
Then went to the washroom, washed his face… and came back to eat his meal silently.

That night…  
Kai lay on his bed, staring at the ceiling.  
The same question kept swirling in his mind —  

“What is there… on Subway Platform 13?”
