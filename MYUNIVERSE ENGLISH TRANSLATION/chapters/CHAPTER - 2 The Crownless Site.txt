Kai reached his house... running.  
His breaths were sharp and fast.  
For the first time in his life, he had seen that much blood all at once — and he was still in shock.  
His hands were still trembling.  

Kai immediately turned on his PC and searched on Zoggle:  
**“A Broken Crown”**  

Only one link appeared in front of him:  
**“The Crownless”**  

Without thinking, Kai clicked on it.  
And the next second...  
his entire desktop went black.  

Kai froze.  

Two seconds later, two black eyes appeared in the center of the screen.  
The eyes slowly opened...  
as if someone inside the screen was looking at him.  

Kai’s heartbeat got even faster.  
For two seconds, those eyes just stared at him...  
Then slowly closed.  

Kai stepped back from his chair.  
“What is this...” he said softly.  

He quickly drank some water...  
And just then, white text started typing itself on the desktop:  

**“Welcome to Broken Crown.”**  

Kai was still staring at that line when —  
the screen glitched.  

The website suddenly crashed.  
Or was it *made* to crash?  

Kai couldn’t understand anything.  
He tried to open the website again...  
But this time, only one line appeared:  

**“This site can’t be reached.”**  

The room was silent.  
Only the sound of the CPU fan was audible.  

Kai now understood...  
this was no ordinary card.  
This was something else entirely.
