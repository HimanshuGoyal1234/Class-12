Kai goes to school the next day.

Ryo comes up to him and says—  
“So, bro, how are things?”

Kai adjusts his bag on his shoulder and speaks softly—  
“I’m fine…”

Ryo looks at him suspiciously.  
“Tell me honestly, bro… you okay, right?”

Kai forces a smile.  
“Yeah, man… just a bit tired.”

Ryo laughs and says—  
“Then come on, let’s get to class. We’re already late anyway.”

Kai,  
“Yeah, coming…”

They’re both walking down the school corridor.  
But Kai’s attention is elsewhere.  

That same name keeps circling in his mind—  
“Baakaa…”  
“Why did he scream like that…”  
“What’s the connection between those two…”

Ryo shakes his shoulder.  
“Oi.”  
“Kai.”

Kai snaps back to reality.  
“Huh?!”

Ryo,  
“Dude, look ahead.”

Kai looks forward…  
And freezes for a second.

Near the school gate, his crush is standing.  
Black hair.  
Puffy cheeks.  
And she’s looking at her phone with a slight smile.

Kai slowly starts blushing.

Ryo grins.  
“Oh, that’s your crush, isn’t it?”

Kai instantly panics.  
“No, bro — shut up—”

Ryo grabs his hand and drags him straight toward her.  
“HEY!”

Yuna.  
“Hmm?”

Ryo casually smiles.  
“I know you don’t know me, but I know you. Your name’s Yuna, right?”

The girl gets confused and tries to hold back a small laugh.  
“Umm… yeah, my name is Yuna Mori.”

Kai starts blushing even harder hearing her name.

Ryo,  
“Cool.”  
“Anyway, my name’s Ryo…”  
“…and this is Kai.”

Kai awkwardly waves.  
“H-hi…”

The girl smiles softly.

Just then, the school bell rings.

“Tch — we’re late!”  
Ryo yells.

All three start running quickly toward the school building.

As soon as they enter the classroom, almost all the seats are full.  
Only 3 seats are left empty.

Kai quietly sits down in the seat next to Yuna.  
His heart is literally racing.

Then his eyes fall on Ryo.  
Ryo gives him a thumbs up.

-----FLASHBACK-----
When they were running through the corridor—
Ryo secretly takes out his phone and messages in the group chat:  
“Only 3 benches should be empty in class.”

The reply comes instantly—  
“OK”
-----FLASHBACK END-----

Kai realizes what happened.  
He turns his face away in embarrassment.

Yuna looks around the classroom and says—  
“Good thing the teacher hasn’t arrived yet…”

Kai says in a nervous tone—  
“Yeah… otherwise we’d be done for.”

They both let out a small laugh.

Just then, the classroom door opens.  
The teacher walks in.

“Good morning, students.”  
“Today we’ll be studying Applications of Derivatives.”

The entire class’s mood sinks.

15 minutes later—  
Yuna is completely bored.  
She rests her head on the desk.

Kai is watching her from the side.  
Her hair has fallen over her face.

Kai awkwardly looks the other way.

After a while, Yuna sits up straight and asks softly—  
“Don’t you ever get bored?”

Kai blurts out without thinking—  
“Not today.”

Yuna raises an eyebrow.  
“What’s so special today?”

Kai instantly panics.  
“N-nothing…”

The bell rings.  
First period is over.

Just then, the classroom door opens—  
“BHABHI NAMASTE!!”

The whole class bursts into laughter.

Yuna freezes.  
“WHAT?!”

Kai’s face literally turns red.  
“Dude, are you crazy?!”  
“Don’t call her bhabhi!”

At the door stand Ryo’s friends—  
Tatsuya Rei and Daichi Moro.

Tatsuya can’t control his laughter.  
Daichi keeps a serious face and says—  
“We were just showing respect.”

Ryo is standing to the side, winking.

Yuna says with an annoyed expression—  
“You guys are weird.”

Ryo, acting in an angry tone—  
“Aeee… don’t call her bhabhi…”
