Kai remained frozen.

The blood-soaked man placed a hand on his shoulder and turned him toward the subway train.  
The other three candidates were also looking at Kai.

Just then, a subway train arrived.  
The blood-soaked man gestured for them to board.  

Those four candidates sat inside the subway.  
The man in the blood-soaked white shirt sat across from them all.  

The train doors slowly closed.  
And at that exact second—  
all the lights of Platform 13 went out.  

Only the dim white lights inside the subway were still on.  

Kai sat near the window…  
but nothing was visible outside.  
As if the train was moving somewhere else entirely.  

The man with the blood-soaked white shirt sat calmly in front of them.  
The dried blood on his clothes had turned dark red now.  
But there wasn’t a single emotion on his face.  

He was observing the four candidates.  
One by one.  
As if judging them.  

The silence inside the train was so heavy that only the metallic vibration of the tracks could be heard.  

Then the girl in the hoodie broke the silence.  
“Why have we been brought here?”  

No answer.  

The scar-knuckle guy got irritated.  
“Oi. If this is a game, just say it straight.”  

The man in the white shirt slowly looked at him.  
Just looked.  

And the next second—  

CRACK.  

The scar-knuckle guy fell off his seat.  
Blood started dripping from his nose.  
No one had even seen him move.  

Kai’s heartbeat instantly spiked.  

“Rule number one,” the white-shirted man finally spoke.  
“Until you’re given permission… don’t ask questions.”  

Silence fell over the compartment again.  

The candidate in the white mask chuckled softly.  
“Interesting…”  

Kai heard his voice for the first time.  
Calm.  
But creepy.  

The train suddenly jerked.  
Lights began to flicker.  
For a second, the entire compartment turned red.  

And in that one second…  
Kai saw something outside the window.  

In the darkness.  
A man hitting an axe into someone’s chest.  

Kai recoiled in shock.  

The lights returned to normal.  

“You saw it too?” the hoodie girl asked softly.  

Kai couldn’t answer.  

The white-shirted man smiled slightly.  
“Good.”  
“That means you noticed something inside the train.”  

Kai almost whispered.  
“The train…?”  

The white-shirted man stood up from his seat.  
And stood in the center of the compartment.  

“Beneath Noir Vale, there’s another city.”  
“Where rulers don’t control gangs…”  
“Monsters do.”  

The train slowly began to decelerate.  

The speaker system glitched.  

“Next Stop…”  
“The Crownless Arena.”  

And at that moment—  
from the next compartment of the subway…  

A girl’s loud scream was heard.  

“Aaaaah!!!!”
