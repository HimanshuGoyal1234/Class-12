A boy stood a little distance away, watching them.

Kane looked toward him and said,  
**“Baakaa.”**

The boy approached.  
“Yeah, Kane… tell me.”

Kane,  
“Take these three there.”

Baakaa looked at all three in a dangerously unsettling way.  
Kai’s heartbeat quickened.  

Ren stared back at him with the same intensity as Baakaa was looking at him.  
Mira glared at him.  

It felt like the three of them were about to fight Baakaa…  
and Kane with the huge guy.  

Just then, Kai’s phone rang.  

Kane looked toward him.  

Kai,  
“Sorry… mom’s call.”  

Kai cut the call.  

Kane ignored it.  

Baakaa led them inside a narrow alley.  
Strange eyes were fixed on them from all sides… as if everyone was judging them.  

Then, without looking back, Baakaa said—  
**“One piece of advice…”**  
**“…don’t try to stay normal here for too long.”**

Kai spoke irritably—  
“What do you mean ‘stay normal’?”

Baakaa let out a small laugh.  
“Normal people don’t survive long here.”

Mira narrowed her eyes at Baakaa.  
“So everyone here is a psycho?”

Baakaa didn’t even stop walking.  
“No.”  
“Just broken.”

At the end of the alley, an old building appeared.  
Above it, a red neon sign blinked—  

**BLACK DOG**

From inside came loud music and the shouting of people.  

Kai said softly,  
“Is this place a bar or a fighting pit…”

Baakaa pushed the door open.  
“Go inside. You’ll find out.”

As soon as they entered—  
The smell of smoke, alcohol, and mixed blood spread through the air.  
Betting slips lay scattered on the floor.  
In the corner, two guys sat badly injured.  
And in the center…  
A crowd stood forming a circle.

**“MOVE!! MOVE!!”**

The crowd shouted loudly.  
In the middle of the circle, two men were fighting.  
One was a thin boy, repeatedly dodging punches.  
The other, a huge guy, was trying to catch him.

**“KILL HIM!!”**

The crowd went even crazier.  
Kai watched silently.  
Mira’s expression was serious.  
Ren just observed.

**BAM.**

The huge guy’s punch landed directly on the other boy’s face.  
He fell straight to the floor.  
Blood slowly began trickling from his mouth.

The crowd cheered.  
**“FINISH IT!!”**

The huge guy was about to climb on top of him—  
Then Baakaa spoke.

**“Stop.”**

The entire crowd’s noise died down in an instant.  
The huge guy immediately stepped back.  

Kai noticed…  
Everyone was afraid of Baakaa.

Baakaa slowly walked into the center of the fight circle.  
The boy lying on the ground was barely breathing.  

Baakaa bent down beside him.  
“Still alive?”

The boy gave a weak smile.  
“Unfortunately…”

Baakaa grabbed his hair and lifted his face.  
“Good.”  
“That means you can be useful.”

Kai’s expression changed.  
“Useful?”

Baakaa turned to look at the newcomers.  
“Platform 13 isn’t a charity.”  
“Here, every person is used for some purpose.”

Mira said irritably,  
“And if someone refuses?”

The room suddenly went quiet.  
Baakaa smiled slowly.  
“Then people don’t survive.”

Ren chuckled beneath his mask.  
“I’m starting to like this place.”

Kai looked at him in disbelief.  
“You’re seriously excited?”

Ren kept staring at the fight circle.  
“Here, everyone stops wearing fake masks.”

Baakaa’s gaze fixed on Ren.  
Two seconds of silence.  
Then—  
**“You seem interesting.”**

Just then, footsteps were heard from the upstairs balcony.  
The entire bar’s atmosphere instantly tensed up.

Kai slowly looked up.  

A tall man stopped by the railing.  
Black shirt.  
Hands in his pockets.  
Face half in shadow.  

And his eyes… were directly fixed on Kai.
