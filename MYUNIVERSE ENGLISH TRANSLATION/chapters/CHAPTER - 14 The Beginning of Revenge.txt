Kane says,  
“Platform Number 13… 7 PM.”

Ryo speaks slowly,  
“I need power…”

Kane smiles faintly.  
“Then come.”

Saying this, Kane leaves.

A while later, Ryo returns home.  
In his hand are 4 bottles — from a distance, they look like alcohol.  
He goes to put them in the freezer — and then it’s revealed: they’re not alcohol. They’re milk bottles.

Ryo stands in front of the mirror and speaks slowly —  
“REVENGE…  
Revenge for my love…”

Both anger and pain are clearly visible in his eyes.

Scene shifts.

Tatsuya is on the phone with some unknown person.  
A voice from the other side says —  
“Meet on Street Number 10.”

The call ends.  
Tatsuya silently puts on his black jacket, then a mask, and leaves home.

Street Number 10.  
As soon as he arrives, Tatsuya sees those same 5 men.  
This is their area.

One of them says with a laugh —  
“Boss… I feel like doing that again.”

The boss’s face instantly fills with anger.  
“You bastard… don’t you understand?”  
“That girl committed suicide… after what we did to her.”

He clenches his fist.  
“I don’t want to do anything like that again.”

Another man says, laughing —  
“But boss… if you don’t, then who will?”

The boss suddenly stands up.  
“Enough.”  
“After today, we won’t do anything like this again.”  
“And… we will surrender ourselves.”

Hearing this, Tatsuya is shocked.  
He thinks from the darkness —  
*“Did they find out about me…?”*  
*“No… if they had, they wouldn’t be talking so casually…”*

Tatsuya quietly slips away.

On the way, he runs into Kane.

Kane looks at his mask and black outfit and laughs.  
“You look quite dangerous…”  
“Did you come back from killing someone?”

Tatsuya starts walking away without answering.

Then Kane calls from behind —  
“Hey, kid… check your hoodie pocket.”

Tatsuya doesn’t stop. He heads straight home.

Reaching home, he removes his mask and says in frustration —  
“Man… I just wanted to have some fun…”

Just then, he remembers Kane’s words.  
“Hey, kid… check your pocket.”

Tatsuya immediately checks his hoodie pocket.  
Inside is a black card.

He says in surprise —  
“How… did this get into my pocket?”

The camera slowly zooms in on the card.  
Written on it in silver letters —  
“THE BLACK CARD”

6:32 PM.  
Tatsuya sits on his bed, staring at the black card.  
Room light is off.  
Only the orange sunlight from the window falls on the card.

“THE BLACK CARD…”

Kane’s smile flashes in his mind again and again.

Just then, the phone vibrates.  
A message from an unknown number:

*“Platform Number 13.  
7:00 PM.  
Come alone.”*

Tatsuya’s throat goes dry.

On the other side…  
Ryo sits quietly in his room.

On the table lies Suzu’s photo.  
Next to that photo — the same ring.

Ryo softly touches the photo.  
“I won’t be weak anymore…”

Now, less tears in his eyes…  
and more anger.

6:58 PM.  
The subway station is almost empty.  
The rain has started again.

Tatsuya descends the stairs in a hoodie.  
His heartbeat is getting faster.

The station map officially shows only 12 platforms.  
But…  
today again, the same thing happens.

A flickering board.  
“13”

Tatsuya slowly starts walking toward it.

As soon as he reaches the bottom, he freezes.  
Three people are already standing there.

A guy in a white mask.  
A girl in a black hoodie.  
And…  
Ryo.

Tatsuya, shocked —  
“RYO?!”

Ryo slowly looks at him.  
But his expression is completely different.  
Blank.  
Cold.  
As if he has already made a decision.

Just then, footsteps echo through the darkness.

*Tak…*  
*Tak…*  
*Tak…*

Kane emerges from the shadows.  
White shirt.  
Hands in pockets.  
Grey eyes fixed directly on both of them.

Kane smiles faintly.

“Welcome back…”  
“To Platform Number 13.”
