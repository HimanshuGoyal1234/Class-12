The station map only showed 12 platforms.  
Still…  

When Kai descended the last staircase,  
a dark hallway lay in front of him.  
And at the end of that hallway—  

**“13”**  
was painted.  

A dim red light kept blinking.  

The platform was completely empty.  
No guards.  
No passengers.  
Only cold wind.  

And near the tracks…  
three people stood.  
Each with black cards in their hands.  

A tall girl in a hoodie.  
A guy with scars on his knuckles.  
And the third…  
wearing a white mask.  

Kai slowly walked toward them.  

Just then, the station speakers turned on again.  

**“Welcome… Crownless Candidates.”**  

The lights switched off for a second.  
And when they came back on—  
in the center of the platform…  
stood that man in the blood-soaked white shirt.  

Cold grey eyes.  
Same smile.  

“12:14,” he said softly.  
“Perfect timing.”
