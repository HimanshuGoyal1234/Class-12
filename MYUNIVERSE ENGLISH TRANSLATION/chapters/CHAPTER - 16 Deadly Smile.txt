Baakaa leads Ryo inside a narrow corridor.  
The walls have scratches and dried bloodstains.

Tatsuya shouts—  
**“RYO! Hey, stop!”**

Baakaa says without turning around—  
“Only the participant goes inside.”

**DHAMM!!**  
The metal gate slams shut.

Tatsuya punches the gate in anger.  
“Damn it.”

Ren stands to the side, laughing.  
“Relax… if he’s that weak, he would’ve died anyway.”

Mira glares at him.  
“Do you think it’s necessary to be creepy every single time?”

Inside…  
Ryo enters a huge circular arena.  
On the upper balconies, people are standing.  
Everyone is betting.

“NEW GUY!”  
“How many minutes will he survive?”

In the center, a spotlight turns on.  
Ryo’s eyes slowly adjust…  
And standing in front of him—  
a man.  
Tall.  
Muscular.  
A stitched scar across his face.

He grins.  
“First fight?”

Ryo quietly says—  
“Yeah.”

“Then here’s some advice…”  
“There are no rules here.”

**CLANG!!**  
The gate locks.

An announcement echoes—  
**“Fight begins.”**

The next second—  
The opponent charges at him.  
Ryo barely dodges.

The crowd cheers.  
“HAHAHAHA!”  
“THIS ONE’S GONNA DIE!”

Ryo breathes heavily.  
A flash in his mind—  
Suzu crying…  
“Ryo…”

Ryo’s eyes slowly turn serious.

The man swings a roundhouse kick—  
but this time…  
Ryo dodges and grabs his neck.

Silence falls over the entire arena.

Baakaa smiles.  
“Good…”

The opponent begins to smirk.  
“Now it gets fun…”

Both their faces twist into deadly smiles.

**“Hahahahaaaaaaa…”**
