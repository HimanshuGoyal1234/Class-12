The match ends, and red lights begin to blink across the entire arena.

**"WINNER — RYO KONJI."**

The crowd is still screaming like madmen.  
Some are repeating his name…  
others are calling him a monster.

Ryo stands breathing heavily, staring at the opponent lying on the ground.  
His hands are still trembling slightly.

Just then, Baakaa slowly descends into the arena.  
His heavy footsteps echo across the floor.

*THAK…*  
*THAK…*  
*THAK…*  

He stops beside the unconscious opponent…  
then looks at Ryo.

"Hmm…"  
Baakaa gives a faint smile.  
"Not bad for a first fight."

Ryo asks in a cold expression—  
"Now what?"

Baakaa looks directly into his eyes and says—  
"Now you're officially inside."

Tatsuya watches from the balcony.  
He now feels a strange fear.  
This was the same Ryo who made stupid jokes at school…  
but the guy standing below felt like someone else entirely.

Suddenly, the sound of clapping comes from the upper balcony.

*CLAP…*  
*CLAP…*  
*CLAP…*  

Everyone looks up.  
The man in the black shirt stands near the railing.  
A dangerous smile is on his face.

"Interesting."

Then he jumps down from the railing.

Pure silence falls over the arena.  
Even Baakaa goes quiet.  
Everyone lowers their gaze…  
as if that man is some kind of monster here.

Ryo stands staring at him.

The black-shirt man stops in front of him.  
"You want revenge?"

Ryo answers without hesitation—  
"Yes."

"How much?"

Ryo’s eyes are filled with rage.  
"As much as it takes."

The black-shirt man smiles.  
"Wrong answer."

Ryo looks at him in confusion.  
Then the man pulls a folded paper from his pocket…  
and tosses it onto Ryo’s chest.

Ryo opens the paper.  
Inside are 5 photos.  
The same 5 men.  
The ones from Street Number 10.

Ryo’s breath stops for a second.

The black-shirt man leans close to his ear and says slowly—  
"Revenge here isn’t taken with just feelings…"  
"…people die here."

Ryo grips the photos tightly.  
His knuckles turn white.

Then Kane speaks from the side—  
"But there’s a problem."

Ryo looks toward him.  
Kane smiles.

"One of those 5 men is already dead."

An eerie silence falls over the entire arena.

Tatsuya whispers slowly—  
“…What?”

Kane says, "Yeah, he’s dead."

Tatsuya says, "But… they were going to surrender."

Kane says, "Yeah, he was on his way to the police station… but how do you know that?"

Kane says, "No way."

Ryo mutters, "Motherf—"

Mira says, "These weaklings get crushed by anyone."
