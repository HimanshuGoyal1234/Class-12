From inside the train tracks, a slow metallic sound begins to echo—

Grrrrrrrrrrr

An old black train emerges from the darkness.  
Its headlights are weak…  
as if they could go out any moment.

As soon as the train stops, the doors open automatically.  
Inside: completely empty.

Kane speaks slowly.  
“Get in.”

Tatsuya says in an irritated tone—  
“First, tell me… why are we here?”

Kane doesn’t even look at him.  
“Revenge.”

1 second of silence.

Ryo quietly steps inside the train.

Tatsuya keeps staring at him, then says—  
“Ryo… are you seriously going with them?”

Ryo says without turning around—  
“What do I have left?”

Inside the train, the lights begin to flicker.  
Mira is observing Tatsuya from inside her hoodie.  
Ren chuckles beneath his mask.  
“Interesting…”

Tatsuya stares at Ren with emotionless eyes.  
Ren slowly tilts his head.  
Tension begins to rise.

Then—

DHAMM!!!

The train door suddenly shuts.

An announcement echoes—  
“Next Stop…”  
“The Crownless Arena.”

The train starts moving.  
Outside the window: only darkness.  
Pure black.

Tatsuya slowly approaches Kane.  
“Who are you people?”

Kane smiles faintly, leaning back in his seat.  
“Us?”  
“We are the ones… rejected by the normal world.”

Ryo asks quietly—  
“Will I get power there?”

Kane looks at him.  
“No.”  
“There… your weakness will die.”

Ryo’s fist slowly clenches.

Then, for one second, the train lights go completely off.  
Pure darkness fills the compartment.

And in that darkness…  
Tatsuya hears someone whisper—  

“Weak…”

After the whisper echoes through the darkness, the train lights turn back on.  
Tatsuya immediately spins around—  
but no one is in the compartment.

His heartbeat quickens.

Ren chuckles softly beneath his mask.  
“You heard it, didn’t you?”

Tatsuya sits down next to Kane.  
Mira and Ren both look on in shock—  
because Kane doesn’t like anyone sitting next to him.  

But he ignores it.

Kane stares at the darkness outside the window and says—  
“Platform 13 gets inside your mind.”

Ryo sits silently.  
In his mind, only Suzu’s voice keeps echoing—  
“Weak…”

The train slowly begins to decelerate.

Announcement:  
“Welcome… to the dead zone.”

The train stops.  
As the doors open, cold wind rushes inside.

Outside is an entire underground city.  
Neon lights.  
Broken buildings.  
And on every wall: the same symbol —  
Broken Crown.

Tatsuya speaks softly—  
“This place… really does exist…”

Just then, Baakaa approaches from the front.  
“Hahahaha… new toys are here.”

Ryo glares at him.

Baakaa stops next to Ryo.  
“Revenge looks good in your eyes.”

Ryo clenches his fist.  
“I need power.”

Baakaa smiles.  
“You’ll get it.”

Then a voice comes from behind—  
“First… survive.”

Everyone turns around.

The man in the black shirt stands on the railing.  
The same man… who had looked at Kai before.  

His eyes are fixed directly on Ryo.

“Throw him into the arena.”

Tatsuya says,  
“Now?”

Baakaa laughs.  
“There’s an entry test here.”

Ryo asks quietly—  
“If I win?”

The black-shirt man smiles slowly.  
“Then you won’t remain a normal human anymore.”
