Kai speaks slowly—  
“Th… that scream… whose was it?”

The girl in the hoodie laughs lightly.  
“Scared already?”

The man in the white shirt.  
“Huh… you hear screams like that here every day.”

The masked candidate looks around.  
“So… something interesting happening over there?”

The white-shirted man turns instantly.  
“NO QUESTIONS.”

He steps out onto the platform.  
It was night.  
The road was almost empty.  

For a while, only the sound of footsteps could be heard.  
Then the man in the white shirt stopped.  

“Names.”

The masked guy speaks without hesitation—  
“Ren Kazuki.”

The girl in the hoodie—  
“Mira Vayne.”

The white-shirted man looks toward Kai.  
“And yours?”  
“…Kai. Kai Ren.”

The white-shirted man stares at him for a few seconds.  
Then speaks slowly—  
“Kane Yojiro.”

Silence.

Kai looks softly at Kane.  
“…How many people had you killed?”

“Stopped counting.”

Mira’s expression turns serious.  
Ren smiles faintly beneath his mask.  
“Interesting…”

Kane looks at the three of them.  
“There’s still time.”  
“Turn back now from here.”  
“Because the area ahead…”  
“…even the police don’t enter.”

Kai raises an eyebrow.  
“What’s there?”

Kane starts walking.  
“Monsters.”

The four of them cross the road.  
In front of them was a narrow area of old buildings.  
Broken neon boards.  
Dark alleys.  
Strange people stood on every corner.  
Some were staring directly at the newcomers.

On the wall of a building, written in spray paint—  

NO RULES INSIDE.

Mira speaks softly—  
“This place…”

Kane answers without turning—  
“Noir Vale’s dead zone.”

The moment they step inside—  
the entire atmosphere of the area changes.  

Somewhere in the distance, the sound of a fight could be heard.  
Beneath the NO RULES INSIDE sign, a man lay in a pool of blood.  

And the strangest thing—  
no one seemed to care.

Kai’s expression tightens.

Just then, a huge guy stops right in front of them.  
Scarred face.  
Tattooed neck.  
Heavy body.  

He looks at Kane and smiles.  
“Huh… Kane Yojiro.”

His gaze slowly shifts to Kai and the other two.  

“New toys?”
