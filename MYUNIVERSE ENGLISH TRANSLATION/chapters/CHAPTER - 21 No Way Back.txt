Next morning…  
Kai enters through the school gate.  
Today the weather is unusually cloudy.  

In the hallway, students are talking normally…  
but Kai feels strangely uneasy.  
As if something is off.  

He walks into his classroom—  
and freezes.  

Ryo is sitting on his seat. And Tatsuya is sitting on the seat behind Ryo.  
But today, something is different.  

His messy hair…  
his swollen right eye…  
and a dark bruise clearly visible on his cheekbone.  

Kai immediately goes to him.  
**“ABE BRO?!”**  
“What have you done to yourself?”  

Ryo slowly looks at him…  
then gives a fake smile.  
“Fell down the stairs.”  

Kai instantly says—  
“Liar.”  

2 seconds of silence.  
Ryo looks away.  
“Just leave it.”  

Just then, Yuna and Naomi also enter the class.  
Naomi is shocked.  
“Ryo?! What happened to your eye?”  

Yuna, “Where have you been for so many days?”  

Ryo, irritated, puts his head down.  
“Why is everyone playing detective today?”  

An awkward silence falls over the classroom.  

Then—  

**KKKRRRTTTTT—**  
The school speakers suddenly glitch.  
Everyone’s attention goes up.  

Through the static, a broken voice echoes—  
**“Attention Students…”**  
**“…please avoid River Side Area after sunset.”**  

River Side.  
Ryo thinks to himself, *“That same place where Kane mentioned the dead body.”*  

Students look at each other in confusion.  
“What happened at River Side?”  
“Some crime?”  

The speaker suddenly returns to normal.  

Ryo quietly grips his desk tightly.  
His knuckles turn white.  

Kai asks softly—  
“…Ryo?”  

But this time, Ryo doesn’t answer.  

Ryo gets up from his seat.  
Without looking at anyone, he walks out of the classroom.  

Kai watches him leave.  
“He’s definitely hiding something…”  

Silence in the corridor.  

Ryo goes straight to the washroom.  
Turns on the tap.  
Splashes cold water on his face.  

Then he stands in front of the mirror, staring at himself.  
His swollen eye…  
His exhausted face…  
And in his mind, those memories keep playing again and again.  

Ryo slowly clenches his palm.  
**“As soon as those four come out of jail…”**  
**“…I will take their lives.”**  

Then—  

**CLICK.**  
The washroom door opens.  

Ryo immediately falls silent.  
In the mirror’s reflection, someone is looking at him.  

Tatsuya is standing there.  

Ryo starts washing his hands.  

Tatsuya says, “Ryo…”  

Ryo, “Hmm.”  

Tatsuya, “You also know martial arts. You never told me.”  

Ryo, “I don’t know it. Just watched it on Learn-Tube.”  

Tatsuya, “Oh… Don’t you think Kai is going to get caught up in this?”  

Ryo, “Yeah… probably.”  

Tatsuya, “Hmm… what should we do?”  

Ryo smiles faintly.  
“Make him feel fear… so that he tries to become powerful.”  

Tatsuya, “How…”  

Suddenly, a guy enters the washroom.  
**“Tee tee tee teee… tu ru ru ruuu… huu huu…”**  
(Singing a FUNK song)
