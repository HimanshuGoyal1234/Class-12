The city was called Noir Vale.  
During the day, it was normal.  
At night, it was an animal.  

Here, fights weren’t just for winning — they were for survival.  
Every area had its own ruler.  
Under every ruler, there were gangs.  
And under every gang were the people… who just wanted to stay alive.  

But one rumor was the same everywhere:  
“If someone stands alone on subway platform 13 after midnight… either they’re about to die, or they’re about to become a legend.”  

17-year-old Kai Ren didn’t believe in legends.  
For him, life was simple — attend school, work a part-time job, and avoid fights.  
But problems didn’t avoid him.  

One night, as he was closing up the convenience store and heading home, he saw a guy in the middle of the road.  
White shirt soaked in blood.  
Face hidden in shadows.  
And around him… 11 bodies.  
Dead or unconscious — hard to tell.  

Kai froze.  
The guy slowly turned toward him.  
Cold grey eyes.  

“What’s the time?” he asked.  

Kai’s throat went dry.  
“12… 12:14.”  

The guy smiled slightly.  
“I’m late.”  

He pulled a black card from his pocket and placed it in Kai’s hand.  
On the card was just one symbol —  
A broken crown.  

Then, the next second…  
Police sirens.  

Kai shut his eyes for just a moment.  
And when he opened them again —  
That guy was gone.  
Just a wet road… blood… and that black card still in his hand.  

After that night, Kai understood —  
In Noir Vale, once some doors open… they never close again.
