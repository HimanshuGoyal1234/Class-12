The next morning...  
Kai had barely slept.  
There were dark circles under his eyes...  
and his mind was still stuck in last night’s scene.  

Even in the school hallway, he felt strange.  
As if someone was watching him.  
Every time he turned a corner...  
he felt like someone in a white shirt was standing there.  
But no one was ever there.  

During lunch break, his friend Ryo Konji came up to him.  
“Bro, you okay?”  
“Yeah.”  
“Liar.”  

Kai didn’t reply.  
Ryo glanced at his hand.  
The black card.  

“What’s that?”  

Kai instantly pulled his sleeve down.  
“Nothing.”  

Ryo stared at him, confused.  

Just then, the school speakers suddenly glitched.  
*kkkkrrrttt—*  

Pure silence fell over the cafeteria.  
Through the static, a distorted voice said:  

“Platform 13... midnight... one chance.”  

And then the speakers returned to normal.  

The students started laughing.  
“Who pulled a prank?”  
“That was creepy, bro.”  

But Kai’s face had turned pale.  
Because he had heard it clearly.  
And he knew —  
this wasn’t a prank.  

That night...  
11:47 PM.  
The rain had started again.  

Kai stood at the entrance of the subway station.  
That black card was in his pocket.  
His mind was telling him to go back.  
But his feet... were walking down.  

Platform 13 didn’t officially exist.
